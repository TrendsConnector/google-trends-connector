- Using TrendsConnector, show me 12-month Google Search growth for electric vehicles.
- What's trending on Google right now in Beauty and Fashion?
- Compare Google search interest in "ozempic" and "wegovy" over 5 years.

Start with "Using TrendsConnector" so your AI uses the connector instead of a web search.

## What it covers

- Google search volume
- Google image search
- Google News search
- Trending searches

| Data | Ask with | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| History and growth | a keyword: any keyword or phrase | Google search volume |
| History and growth | a keyword: any keyword or phrase | Google image search volume |
| History and growth | a keyword: any keyword or phrase | Google News search volume |
| Live board: Google Trends | no keyword | Top trending search terms on Google right now |
| Live board: Google Trends by Category | no keyword | Google trending searches split by topic board |

## How to ask

Pass any keyword or phrase. Web search, image search, and Google News search are separate series. The main trending board needs no keyword. The category board needs a category name, such as Games.

**Good to know:** This is search interest, not Google Ads keyword volume and not a count of purchases. Google Shopping is its own connector.

## FAQ

**Can I separate Google web, image, and news search?**  
Yes. Web search, image search, and Google News search are separate series on this connector.

**Does this include Google Shopping?**  
No. Shopping search volume is on the Google Shopping connector.

**Is the Google Trends connector free?**  
Yes. The free plan has 100 requests a month and includes every source. No credit card. Paid plans start at $19 a month for 1,000 requests.

**Which AI apps work with it?**  
Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Claude Code, Grok, Muse, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Windsurf and any app that supports MCP connectors.
